Words Into Motion

Words Into Motion is an educational Google Slides™ add-on for elementary teachers.

Purpose Of Words Into Motion

The purpose of Words Into Motion is to help teachers make elementary math word problems easier for students to understand by turning each problem into simple visual storyboard slides.

What Words Into Motion Does

Teachers enter a single-step addition or subtraction word problem. Words Into Motion creates a storyboard that introduces the character, object, starting quantity, change, and final answer. Teachers can review the storyboard before inserting the generated slides into a Google Slides™ presentation.

Who Uses Words Into Motion

Words Into Motion is intended for elementary teachers and education professionals who want to create visual scaffolds for classroom math instruction.

Setup

Open Google Slides™, choose Extensions > Words Into Motion > Open Words Into Motion, then save your Groq API key in Settings before generating your first storyboard.
