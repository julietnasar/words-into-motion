Words Into Motion Support

For help, email wordsintomotion@gmail.com.

What to Include

  • What you were trying to do.
  • The exact error message, if any.
  • Whether you had already saved a Groq API key.
  • Whether the issue occurred during generation or slide insertion.
  • A sample non-private word problem that reproduces the issue.

Do not send student names, student records, API keys, or confidential school information.
